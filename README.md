The materials in this repository are from the "Introduction to Phylogenomics" workshop that will be held on Tue 1 Dec 2026 at the University of Wollongong. The workshop has been organised in conjunction with Evolution in the Gong, a joint meeting of the Australasian Evolution Society and the Society of Australian Systematic Biologists. 

This half-day workshop will provides an introduction to phylogenetics and phylogenomics, including practical exercises based on the software MEGA, IQ-TREE, and ASTER. 

Workshop instructors will be Simon Ho and Manuela Cascini (University of Sydney) and Rob Lanfear (Australian National University).

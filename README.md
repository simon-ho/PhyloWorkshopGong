The materials in this repository are from the [Introduction to Phylogenomics](https://ausevo.com/workshop/) workshop that will be held on Tue 1 Dec 2026 at the University of Wollongong. The workshop has been organised in conjunction with [Evolution in the Gong](https://ausevo.com/conference/), a joint meeting of the Australasian Evolution Society and the Society of Australian Systematic Biologists. 

This half-day workshop will provide an introduction to phylogenetics and phylogenomics, including short practical exercises based on the software _MEGA_, _IQ-TREE_, and _ASTER_. Workshop instructors will be Simon Ho and Manuela Cascini (University of Sydney) and Rob Lanfear (Australian National University). 

The practical exercises require the following software:
- [_MEGA_](https://www.megasoftware.net/) version 12.1
- [_IQ-TREE_](https://iqtree.github.io/) version 3.1.4
- [_ASTER_](https://github.com/chaoszhang/ASTER)
- [_FigTree_](https://github.com/rambaut/figtree/releases) version 1.4.4
